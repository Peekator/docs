The Responses table in Peekator shows every submission your survey has collected. Each row is one respondent and each column represents a question. You can sort, search, and drill into individual responses directly from the dashboard.

## Browse the responses table

Open any survey and click **Responses** in the top navigation. The table loads with one row per submission. Column headers show question titles or short labels. Use the horizontal scroll to see all questions if your survey is long.

- **Sort** any column by clicking the column header to order responses by that answer.
- **Search** for a specific response using the search bar at the top of the table. The search applies across all text-based answers.

## View an individual response

Click any row in the table to open the full response detail view. This shows every question and the respondent's answer on a single page, along with metadata such as submission time and the distribution channel (email, link, or embed).

## Manage response records

### Mark a response as complete or incomplete

Some submissions arrive in an incomplete state if a respondent did not reach the end of the survey. To update the status, open the response detail view, click the **Status** dropdown, and select **Complete** or **Incomplete**.

### Delete a response

<Warning>
  Deleting a response is permanent and cannot be undone. The record and all associated answers are removed immediately.
</Warning>

To delete a single response, open the response detail view and click **Delete Response**. Confirm the action when prompted. The response is removed from the table and no longer counts toward your summary stats.

## Summary statistics per question

Below the Responses table, Peekator shows aggregate statistics for each question:

- **Multiple choice and single choice**: answer counts and percentage breakdown.
- **Rating scale**: average score, minimum, maximum, and distribution histogram.
- **Open-ended**: total response count and a word cloud of common terms.
- **NPS**: score, Promoter/Passive/Detractor counts, and NPS gauge chart.
