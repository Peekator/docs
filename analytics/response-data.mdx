The response data view in Peekator lets you move beyond aggregate charts and inspect individual respondent records. This is useful when you need to verify specific answers, investigate anomalies, or prepare a detailed review of your dataset before exporting.

## What the response data view shows

Unlike the dashboard charts, which summarise all responses for a question, the response data view displays each respondent's answers as individual records. This gives you a row-level view of your dataset.

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** whether a raw response table view exists in the dashboard and what columns it shows
</Note>

Typically, a response data view includes:

- Respondent identifier or response ID
- Start and completion timestamps
- Answers to each question in the survey
- Any respondent attributes captured during distribution

## How to access response data

To view individual responses:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your survey">
    Go to the survey you want to review and open the **Reporting** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to response data">
    Look for a tab or toggle that switches from the chart dashboard to the response-level view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search records">
    Scroll through the list of responses, or use search and filter options to locate specific records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How this relates to exports

The response data view is a preview of the information you can export. When you download your data in CSV format, you receive the same row-level structure for external analysis. For formatted summaries, use Excel or PowerPoint exports instead. See [Export your survey data](/exports/scheduled-reports) for details on each format.
