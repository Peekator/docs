The Peekator dashboard gives you a live view of incoming response data for any survey that is collecting or has completed. You can find it inside the **Reporting** tab when you open a survey. As responses arrive, the dashboard updates automatically so you can track progress and spot trends without waiting for a full export.

## What the dashboard shows

When you open the dashboard, you see a summary of your survey's performance and a breakdown of results per question.

- **Response count**: the total number of responses received so far
- **Completion rate**: the percentage of started surveys that were finished
- **Charts per question**: visual summaries generated automatically for each question based on its type
- **Response timeline**: a view of when responses arrived, useful for spotting peaks after a campaign send

## When to use the dashboard

Use the dashboard during fieldwork to monitor response quality and volume. It is also the fastest way to share initial findings with stakeholders before producing a formal report.
