The Peekator dashboard gives you a live view of your survey results from the moment responses start arriving. You can see high-level summary stats, explore per-question breakdowns with interactive charts, and browse every individual response in a table without leaving the platform.

<Note>
  The dashboard updates in real time. You do not need to refresh the page to see new responses as they are submitted.
</Note>

## What the dashboard shows

The dashboard is organized into three main areas:

- **Summary stats** at the top: total responses, completion rate, and average time to complete.
- **Per-question charts** in the middle: automatically generated visualizations for each question in your survey.
- **Responses table** at the bottom: one row per respondent with all their answers, sortable and searchable.
