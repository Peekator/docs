Filters let you narrow the data shown in the Peekator dashboard so you can focus on a specific subset of responses. You can filter by answer value, date range, or respondent attributes. This is useful when you want to see how a particular group answered, or when you need to exclude incomplete or test responses from your view.

## How filters work

When you apply a filter, the entire dashboard updates to show only the responses that match your criteria. Charts, counts, and completion rates all recalculate automatically based on the filtered set.

## How to add a filter

To apply a filter to your survey results:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Navigate to the **Reporting** tab inside your survey and select the dashboard view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the filter panel">
    Locate the filter controls, usually near the top of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your filter criteria">
    Select the question, date range, or respondent attribute you want to filter by, then pick the values to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Confirm your selection. The dashboard refreshes to show only matching responses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters vs crosstabs

Filters and crosstabs both help you break down your data, but they serve different purposes.

| Use filters when... | Use crosstabs when... |
| --- | --- |
| You want to explore one group at a time | You want to compare multiple groups side by side |
| You are preparing a filtered export | You need a cross-tabulation table for statistical review |
| You want to temporarily hide certain responses | You want to see how every question varies across a demographic |

For side-by-side comparisons, see [Crosstabs in Peekator](/exports/crosstabs).
