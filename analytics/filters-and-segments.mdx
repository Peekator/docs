Filters let you narrow your survey results to a specific subset of responses. Segments let you save a filter set and reuse it across sessions or compare it side by side with other groups. Both filters and segments apply across the dashboard charts, summary stats, and exports.

## Apply a filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard for your survey">
    Go to the survey's analytics view in Peekator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Filters">
    Click the **Filters** button at the top of the dashboard. The filters panel opens on the right side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a filter condition">
    Click **Add filter** and choose a filter type:

    - **Answer to a question**: show only responses where a specific question has a specific answer.
    - **Date range**: limit results to responses submitted within a chosen date window.
    - **Completion status**: show only complete or only incomplete responses.
    - **Respondent metadata**: filter by channel (email, link, embed) or other metadata if collected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the condition values">
    Select the question, condition (equals, contains, is greater than, etc.), and the value to filter by.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply**. The dashboard instantly refreshes to show only the matching responses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Filters are applied across the entire dashboard: summary stats, all charts, and the responses table all update to reflect the filtered subset. When you export data, active filters carry over to the export.
</Note>

## Combine multiple filters

You can stack multiple filter conditions. By default, all conditions must be true (AND logic). Add as many conditions as you need to narrow your results to a precise segment.

## Save a segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your filters">
    Apply one or more filter conditions as described above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save as segment">
    Click **Save as segment** in the filters panel and give the segment a name, such as `Detractors - Q3` or `EMEA Respondents`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the segment later">
    Saved segments appear in the **Segments** dropdown at the top of the dashboard. Select one to apply it instantly without rebuilding the filter conditions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare segments side by side

<Tip>
  Use segments to compare different audience groups. For example, compare NPS scores between customers who rated ease of use highly and those who rated it poorly to surface what drives loyalty.
</Tip>

To compare two segments, open the segments panel, select a second segment, and enable **Compare mode**. The dashboard shows both segments in parallel, with charts split by segment.

## Remove a filter or segment

To clear all active filters, click **Clear all** in the filters panel. To delete a saved segment, open the segments list, hover over the segment name, and click the delete icon.
