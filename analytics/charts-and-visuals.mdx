Peekator generates charts automatically for every question in your survey dashboard. These visuals update in real time as new responses arrive, giving you an immediate picture of how respondents are answering without needing to build a report manually.

## Available chart types

Charts are generated based on the question type you used in the questionnaire. This means the visual format is chosen to represent the data clearly.

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** full list of chart types per question type
</Note>

Common chart types include:

- **Bar charts**: used for single choice and multiple choice questions to show the distribution of selected options
- **Tables**: used for grid or matrix questions where respondents rated several items on the same scale
- **Pie or donut charts**: may be used for single choice questions with a small number of options
- **Line or area charts**: may appear in timeline or trend views showing responses over time

## How to interact with charts

You can explore the charts directly in the dashboard to dig into the data.

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** exact chart interactions available
</Note>

- Hover over chart segments to see exact counts and percentages
- Click on a segment to filter the entire dashboard by that answer, if filtering by chart selection is supported
- Scroll through the dashboard to review each question's chart in sequence

## Exporting charts

If you need to include charts in a presentation or share them outside Peekator, you may be able to export individual charts or the full dashboard.

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** whether individual chart export is available
</Note>

For a complete export of all charts and data, use the export options described in [Export your survey data](/exports/scheduled-reports).
