This guide takes you from your first login to a live, collecting survey in just a few minutes. You will create a project, add a survey, build a short questionnaire, and share it with respondents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Peekator">
    Open the Peekator login page and enter your email address and password. If you do not have login details yet, contact the Peekator team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    From the main menu, go to **Projects and Campaigns**, then click to create a new project. Give it a clear name that describes your research.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a survey">
    Inside your new project, add a survey. Choose **Survey** mode (the default) and enter a name. The survey card now appears on your project page with a status of **Editing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one question">
    Open the survey and go to the questionnaire builder. Click to add a question, choose a question type, and enter your question text and answer options. Add as many questions as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a distribution channel">
    Go to the **Distribution** tab. Select **Link** to generate a live link immediately. This is the fastest way to start collecting responses.

    <Tip>
      Copy the **Test Link** and open it in a private browser window to preview the full respondent experience before you launch.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Review and launch">
    Return to the questionnaire page and click **Review and launch**. Peekator checks your survey for issues. If everything looks correct, confirm to move the survey into the **Collecting** state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link and monitor responses">
    Copy the **Live Link** from the Distribution page and share it with your audience. Open the survey dashboard to watch responses arrive in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your survey is now live and collecting responses. You can pause, edit, or close it at any time from the survey card or settings page.
