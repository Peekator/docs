This guide takes you from your first login to a live, collecting survey in just a few minutes. You will create a project, add a survey, build a short questionnaire, and share it with respondents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Peekator">
    Open the Peekator login page and enter your email address and password. If you do not have login details yet, contact the Peekator team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    From the main menu, go to **Projects**, then click to create a new project. Give it a clear name that describes your research.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a survey or an AI interview">
    Inside your new project, add a **survey** or an **interview**, and enter a name. The survey card now appears on your project page with a status of **Editing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one question">
    Open the survey and go to the questionnaire builder. Click to add a question, choose a question type, and enter your question text and answer options. Add as many questions as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a distribution channel">
    Go to the **Distribution** tab and choose the method that suits your audience: Link, Panel or Campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the launch step">
    The final step depends on your distribution method:

    - **Link**: Select **Review and launch** to move the survey to **Collecting**.
    - **Panel**: Select **Review and launch** to move the survey to **In review**. The Peekator team launches it after your final approval.
    - **Campaigns**: Once confirmed, the Peekator team updates the survey status and launches the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor responses">
    Once your survey is collecting responses, open the dashboard to monitor incoming responses and results in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your survey is now live and collecting responses. You can pause, edit, or close it at any time from any tab within the survey.
