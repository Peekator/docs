Peekator's AI analysis features help you move from raw data to insights faster. Instead of manually scanning every response or building pivot tables, you can use AI to explore patterns, summarise findings, and organise open-ended text automatically. These features are available in the **AI Analysis** section of the Reporting tab.

## Main AI features

Peekator offers two core AI capabilities for survey analysis:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat with Results" icon="message-circle" href="/ai-analysis/chat-with-results">
    Ask questions about your survey data in plain language and get instant answers with source citations. Includes preset quick actions for common tasks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Auto-coding" icon="tags" href="/ai-analysis/auto-coding">
    Let AI read all open-ended responses and group them into named categories automatically, saving hours of manual coding.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Interview mode note

In Interview mode, the AI Analysis area is called **Chat with Data** and works identically to Chat with Results. The functionality is the same: you ask questions about your discussion guide data and receive AI-generated answers with citations.

## Getting better AI results

<Tip>
  Set business and research objectives when creating your project to give the AI more context for tailored results. The more the AI knows about your goals, the more relevant its summaries and suggestions will be.
</Tip>
