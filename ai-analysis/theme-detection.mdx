Chat with Results is a conversational AI interface built into every survey's reporting section. You type a question about your data in plain language, and the AI reads the full survey results to generate a response. Each answer includes citation chips (Q1, Q2, etc.) showing which survey questions the AI used as sources.

## Start a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open survey reporting">
    Open the survey and go to the **Reporting** or **Dashboard** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Chat with Results">
    Click the **Chat with Results** tab in the reporting navigation. For Interview mode surveys, this tab is labeled **Chat with Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question or use a quick action">
    Enter a question in the text field, or click one of the preset quick action cards to get started immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the response">
    The AI generates a response in the chat window. Citation chips next to the text indicate which questions from your survey were used as the basis for that answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick actions

Preset quick actions are available at the start of every chat to generate common report types in one click.

| Quick action | What it generates |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate AI Summary** | A written overview of the survey's key findings across all questions |
| **Table Suggestions** | Recommended crosstab combinations and analysis angles for your data |
| **Analyze Specific Audience** | A focused analysis of a particular respondent segment |

## Voice input

Click the microphone icon to record a spoken question. Peekator transcribes the audio and submits it as a text message. This is useful when you want to ask longer questions hands-free.

## Chat history

Every chat session is saved automatically. To reopen a past conversation, click the history panel on the left side of the Chat with Results screen. Previous sessions are listed by their AI-generated summary name. You can continue an existing chat or start a new one at any time.

## Understanding citations

The AI response includes citation chips (for example, `Q3`, `Q7`) that indicate which survey questions were used as sources. Click a citation chip to see the full question text. This lets you verify which data the AI drew from when generating its answer.

<Note>
  If any open-ended questions have not been auto-coded yet, a warning appears at the top of the chat screen. Run [Auto-coding](/ai-analysis/sentiment-analysis) on those questions first for more accurate analysis.
</Note>
