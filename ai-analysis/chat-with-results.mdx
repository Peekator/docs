Chat with Results is a conversational AI interface in Peekator where you ask questions about your survey data and receive instant, sourced answers. Instead of filtering charts or exporting data to build pivot tables, you can type or speak a question and let the AI surface insights, patterns, and summaries for you.

You can find Chat with Results in the **AI Analysis** section of the Reporting tab inside any survey.

## Preset quick actions

Peekator provides preset actions to help you get started quickly:

- **Generate AI Summary**: produces a high-level summary of your survey results
- **Table Suggestions**: recommends useful cross-tabulations or breakdowns based on your data
- **Analyse Specific Audience**: focuses the AI on a particular segment or respondent group

## Key features

- **Voice input**: ask questions by speaking instead of typing
- **Chat history**: review previous questions and answers within the session
- **Source citations**: the AI references specific questions and responses so you can verify its answers against the raw data

## How to use Chat with Results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat with Results">
    Go to the **Reporting** tab of your survey and select the **AI Analysis** section, then open **Chat with Results**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Type or speak a question about your data. For example: "What percentage of respondents rated satisfaction above 7?" or "Summarise the main themes in the open-ended feedback."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the answer and sources">
    Read the AI response and check the cited sources to confirm which questions and responses it used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up or refine">
    Ask follow-up questions to dig deeper, or use a preset action to explore a different angle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to use Chat with Results

Use this feature when you need:

- **Quick insights**: answer ad-hoc questions during fieldwork without building a report
- **Exploration**: investigate unexpected patterns or outliers in your data
- **Stakeholder summaries**: generate plain-language summaries to share with non-research audiences

## Testing tip

<Warning>
  Always verify AI answers against the raw data for critical decisions. The AI provides a fast starting point, but human review is essential before using its outputs in client deliverables or strategic decisions.
</Warning>

## Interview mode note

In Interview mode, this feature is called **Chat with Data**. The functionality is identical: you ask questions about your discussion guide data and receive AI-generated answers with citations.
