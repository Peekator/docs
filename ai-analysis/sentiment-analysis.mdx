Sentiment analysis in Peekator automatically reads every open-ended response and classifies it as **Positive**, **Neutral**, or **Negative**. This gives you an immediate read on how respondents feel without having to manually review hundreds or thousands of text answers.

## How sentiment is classified

Peekator's AI evaluates the language and tone of each response individually:

- **Positive**: the response expresses satisfaction, appreciation, enthusiasm, or a favorable opinion.
- **Neutral**: the response is factual, mixed, or does not lean clearly in either direction.
- **Negative**: the response expresses dissatisfaction, frustration, criticism, or an unfavorable opinion.

## View sentiment results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your survey's analytics">
    Go to the survey dashboard and scroll to the open-ended question you want to analyze.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sentiment">
    Click the **Sentiment** tab above the response list for that question. Peekator displays a donut chart showing the proportion of Positive, Neutral, and Negative responses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by sentiment">
    Click any segment of the donut chart (for example, **Negative**) to filter the response list below to show only responses with that sentiment. Read through them to understand the specific concerns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare sentiment across segments

<Tip>
  Create two segments in the [Filters and segments](/analytics/filters-and-segments) panel, then switch between them on the sentiment chart to compare how different groups feel. For example, compare sentiment from enterprise customers versus small business customers.
</Tip>

## Export sentiment data

Sentiment classifications are included when you export to CSV or Excel. Each response row includes a `Sentiment` column with the value `Positive`, `Neutral`, or `Negative`.
