Sentiment analysis classifies open-ended responses as positive, negative, or neutral based on the language respondents use. It helps you quickly gauge the emotional tone of feedback, comments, or verbatim answers without reading every response individually.

## What sentiment analysis does

In general, sentiment analysis:

- Scans open-ended text for positive, negative, and neutral language
- Assigns a sentiment label to each response or segment
- Aggregates results so you can see the overall tone of feedback for a question or topic

## When to use it

Sentiment analysis is useful when you want to:

- Track satisfaction or dissatisfaction from free-text answers
- Compare emotional tone across audience segments or time periods
- Prioritise which responses need detailed human review

## Current status in Peekator

Until product verification confirms how sentiment analysis is delivered, use the following AI features for open-ended analysis:

- **Auto-coding**: groups responses into themes, which may include sentiment-related categories
- **Chat with Results**: ask questions about the tone or sentiment of responses and receive AI-generated summaries with source citations
