Auto-coding in Peekator uses AI to read all open-ended responses and group them into named categories automatically. Instead of reading every text answer and assigning codes by hand, you can let the AI suggest categories, review them, and refine the names before sharing results with stakeholders.

## When to use auto-coding

Auto-coding is most useful when you have:

- **Large volumes of open-ended text**: hundreds or thousands of responses that would take hours to code manually
- **Tight deadlines**: you need initial themes quickly to inform follow-up questions or reporting
- **Exploratory research**: you want to see what themes emerge before designing a formal coding frame

## How to use auto-coding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the relevant question">
    In the dashboard, navigate to the open-ended question you want to code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run auto-coding">
    Select the auto-coding option for that question. The AI reads all responses and generates a set of named categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the categories">
    Inspect the AI-generated category names and the responses assigned to each. Check that the groupings make sense for your research objectives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust category names if needed">
    Rename categories to match your preferred terminology or to align with client reporting standards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating categories for new responses

When new responses arrive after your initial coding run, you can use the **Recode and add new answers** option. This updates the existing categories to include the new responses, so your coding stays current throughout fieldwork.

## Testing tip

<Warning>
  Always review AI-generated categories before sharing results. Check a sample of responses against their assigned categories to confirm the AI has grouped them accurately. AI categories are a starting point, not a final deliverable.
</Warning>
