Peekator's AI features can dramatically speed up insight generation, but they work best when you use them with clear objectives and a consistent review process. Follow these best practices to get accurate, trustworthy results from Chat with Results, Auto-coding, and related tools.

## Set objectives at project level

When you create a project in Peekator, use the AI context fields to set your business and research objectives. The AI uses this context to tailor its summaries, suggestions, and category names to your specific goals. Projects with clear objectives produce more relevant and actionable AI outputs than those without.

## Use Chat with Results for exploration, not final reporting

Chat with Results is designed for rapid exploration and ad-hoc questioning. It is an excellent tool for spotting patterns, generating hypotheses, and preparing talking points. However, always verify its answers against raw data before including them in client reports or strategic decisions.

## Review auto-coded categories before sharing

AI-generated categories from Auto-coding are a starting point, not a finished coding frame. Before you share coded results:

- Read a sample of responses in each category to confirm they belong there
- Rename categories to match your reporting standards or client terminology
- Merge or split categories where the AI has grouped unrelated themes together

## Check source citations for accuracy

Chat with Results includes source citations that reference specific questions and responses. Use these citations to:

- Confirm the AI interpreted the data correctly
- Trace surprising findings back to the raw responses
- Build confidence in the AI output before presenting it to stakeholders

## Test AI features with real data before client delivery

<Warning>
  Before using AI-generated outputs in client deliverables, run the features on a subset of real responses and compare the results to your own manual review. This helps you understand the AI's accuracy for your specific dataset and question types.
</Warning>

## Use Chat with Data in Interview mode

In Interview mode, the equivalent feature is called **Chat with Data**. Use it to analyse discussion guide results with the same conversational approach. The best practices above apply equally: set objectives, verify outputs, and review citations before sharing.
