AI analysis in Peekator works best with a little preparation. These tips help you get more accurate, useful results from both Chat with Results and Auto-coding.

## Prepare your data first

Before using Chat with Results, run [Auto-coding](/ai-analysis/sentiment-analysis) on all open-ended questions. Chat with Results draws on auto-coded categories to give more precise, theme-aware answers. If you skip this step, the AI will still work but a warning will appear and analysis of open-ended text will be less structured.

## Ask specific questions

Broad questions return broad answers. Narrow your question to a specific topic or audience for more useful results.

Instead of asking:

- "What do people think?"
- "What are the results?"

Try asking:

- "What are the top three complaints about the onboarding process?"
- "How do respondents in the 18-34 age group rate the product compared to older groups?"
- "Which features received the most positive feedback?"

## Use quick actions as a starting point

If you are not sure where to begin, click **Generate AI Summary** first. This gives you a broad overview of the findings. From there, ask follow-up questions to go deeper into specific areas that catch your attention.

## Apply filters before asking

Use the [dashboard filters](/analytics/filters-and-segments) to narrow the data to a specific segment before opening Chat with Results. For example, filter to a single market, a specific date range, or a respondent subgroup. The AI will analyze only the filtered responses, giving you segment-specific answers.

## Save important conversations

Chat history is saved per survey and listed by the AI-generated session name. If you want to find a past conversation easily, start it with a descriptive first message (for example, "Analysis for Q4 board review"). The AI uses your messages to name the session in the history list.

<Tip>
  For Interview mode surveys, Chat with Results is labeled **Chat with Data**. The feature works identically.
</Tip>
