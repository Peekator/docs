Distribution is how you make your survey available to respondents. In Peekator, you choose a channel inside the **Distribution** tab of a survey, configure it, and then launch. The platform supports three channel types: sharing a direct link, running a multi-channel campaign, or using a managed panel.

## Distribution channels

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Link sharing" icon="link" href="/distribution/link-sharing">
    Share a live URL or test link directly with respondents. Best for quick tests, social media, or embedding.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Campaigns" icon="megaphone" href="/distribution/campaigns">
    Send the survey via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp to a defined contact list. Best for targeted outreach.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Panel" icon="users" href="/distribution/panel">
    Let Peekator source respondents from a managed panel. Best when you need a specific audience without managing contacts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to set up distribution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Distribution tab">
    Inside your survey, select **Setup**, then click **Distribution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Pick **Link**, **Campaign**, or **Panel** depending on how you want to reach respondents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the channel">
    Fill in the required fields for your chosen channel. Each channel has its own setup steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and launch">
    When you are ready, click **Review and launch**. The survey moves to the appropriate state based on the channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before launching, use the test link to verify logic, screenouts, and quotas. See the [launch checklist](/distribution/launch-checklist) for a full pre-launch review.
</Tip>
