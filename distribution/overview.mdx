Distribution is how you make your survey available to respondents. In Peekator, you choose a channel inside the **Distribution** tab of a survey, configure it, and then launch. The platform supports three channel types: sharing a direct link, running a multi-channel campaign, or using a managed panel.

## Distribution channels

  <Card title="Campaigns" icon="megaphone" href="/distribution/campaigns">

  <Card title="Panel" icon="users" href="/distribution/panel">

  </Card>

Select a channel, complete the relevant settings and select **Save changes**.

## Share a survey link

Choose **Link** to generate a survey URL that you can share directly with respondents.

You will receive two links:

- **Live Link**: use this to collect real responses.
- **Test Link**: use this to preview and test the survey. Test responses are not included in the results.

When ready, **copy** the appropriate link, click on **Review and launch**, and share the link.

Once fieldwork is complete, click on **Pause/Finish Collecting** to stop collecting responses.

## Send a campaign

Choose **Campaign** when you want to invite a contact list by email or SMS.

After saving the distribution setting:

1. Tell the Peekator team which sending channel you want to use.
2. Share any requirements for the campaign template branding, such as a different logo or colour scheme from the survey itself.
3. Click on **Review and launch** to change the survey status to Collecting
4. Open **Campaign** from the top navigation.
5. Create the campaign, upload your contact list and configure the send.

See [Campaigns](/distribution/campaigns) for the full process.

## Recruit through a panel

Choose **Panel** to recruit respondents from a panel provider.

Select one of the following options:

| Panel option | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Panel managed by Peekator** | Use Peekator Panel and manage the project in one place. |
| **Connect Panel** | Use your own external panel. |

### Panel managed by Peekator

Enter your sample requirements, including:

- number of respondents;
- market; and
- target audience.

When you select **Review and Launch**, the survey enters **Review** mode. The Peekator team checks the survey before launching it to the selected panels.

At the end of fieldwork, the team completes a **Data Quality** review and replaces low-quality responses where needed. The survey then moves to **Done** with cleaned results.

### Connect your own panel

Select **Connect Panel** when you want to use an external panel provider. Complete the provider-specific settings shown for that setup, and click on the Review and launch.

See [Panels](/distribution/panel) for the full setup.
