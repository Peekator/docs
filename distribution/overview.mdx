Once your survey is published, you can distribute it through three channels: a shareable link, Campaigns, or Panel. Each channel suits a different audience and collection strategy. You can use multiple channels for the same survey simultaneously.

<Note>
  A survey must have a status of **Published** before it can be distributed. Open the survey builder and click **Publish** to make it available.
</Note>

## Distribution channels

| Channel | Best for | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Link | Open sharing, QR codes, print, or embedding | Peekator generates a URL you share anywhere |
| Campaigns | Reaching your own contacts directly | Upload a contact list and send via email, SMS, or WhatsApp |
| Panel | Recruiting respondents you do not already have | Use a Peekator-managed panel or bring your own |
