Link distribution gives you a direct URL you can share with respondents. It is the fastest way to launch a survey and works well for email invites you send yourself, social posts, website embeds, and quick tests.

## Set up link distribution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Distribution">
    Inside your survey, go to **Setup** and select the **Distribution** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Link">
    Choose **Link** as your distribution channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your links">
    Peekator generates two links for you:

    - **Live Link**: the URL respondents use to submit real responses.
    - **Test Link**: the URL you use to preview and test the survey without recording real data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the live link">
    Click the copy button next to **Live Link** and paste it into an email, chat message, website, or social post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to use link distribution

Use link distribution when:

- You already have your own contact list and do not need Peekator to send messages.
- You want to embed the survey on a website or intranet.
- You are running a quick pilot or internal test.
- You want respondents to share the survey organically.

<Tip>
  Always use the **Test Link** to verify logic, branching, screenouts, and quotas before sharing the live link. Test responses are not saved as real data.
</Tip>
