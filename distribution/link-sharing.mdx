## Set up link distribution

1. In your survey, select **Setup** and open **Distribution**.
2. Choose **Link** as the distribution channel, then select **Save changes**.
3. Peekator generates two URLs:
   - **Live Link** — use this link to collect real survey responses.
   - **Test Link** — use this link to check the survey before launch. Test responses are not included in your live results.
4. Select **Review and Launch** in the top-right corner. Review the complete survey, including the question order and logic, then launch it when you are ready.
5. Copy the **Live Link** and share it through your chosen channel, such as an email, website, social post or internal communication.

## Test before sharing

Use the **Test Link** to complete the survey as a respondent before you launch. Check that:

- Questions and answer options display correctly.
- Conditions, piping and text substitutions work as expected.
- Any required answers or validation rules behave correctly.
- The survey is shown in the right language, where relevant.

You can continue using the Test Link after launch without affecting the live results.

## Pause or finish collection

Once the survey is live, the **Review and Launch** control changes to collection controls:

- **Pause collecting** temporarily stops new responses while keeping the survey available to resume later.
- **Finish collecting** ends data collection when you no longer want to accept responses.

You do not need to return to Survey settings to pause or finish the survey.

## Add information through the link

You can add [query strings](/questionnaire/pre-fill-answers-with-query-strings) to a Live Link to pre-fill answers, such as language or audience, without asking respondents that question in the survey.
