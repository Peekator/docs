Link sharing is the simplest way to distribute your survey. Peekator generates a unique URL for every published survey that you can share through any channel: social media, messaging apps, internal portals, or printed materials using a QR code.

## Get the survey link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Distribute tab">
    In your published survey, click the **Distribute** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Link">
    Click **Link** to see your survey URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Click **Copy link** to copy the URL to your clipboard. Share it anywhere you want respondents to access your survey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link options

### Enable or disable the link

Toggle the link on or off to open or close the survey to new responses at any time. When the link is disabled, respondents who visit the URL see a "survey is closed" message. Existing responses are not affected.

### Set a response limit per link

Enter a number in the **Response limit** field to cap how many responses this link will accept. Once the limit is reached, the link is automatically disabled. This is separate from the overall survey response limit in [Survey settings](/surveys/survey-settings).

### Enable password protection

Turn on **Password protection** and enter a password. Respondents must enter the correct password before they can access the survey. Share the password separately from the link.

### Generate a QR code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Generate QR code">
    In the link distribution panel, click **Generate QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the QR code">
    Download the QR code as a PNG or SVG and place it on printed materials, posters, event signage, or product packaging.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  QR codes work well for in-person events, retail locations, and printed collateral where respondents can scan directly with their phone camera.
</Tip>
