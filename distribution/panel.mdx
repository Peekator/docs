Panel distribution lets you reach respondents outside your own contact list. You can use a panel managed by Peekator, where Peekator sources qualified respondents on your behalf, or bring your own panel if you already manage a pool of respondents.

## Panel options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Peekator-Managed Panel" icon="users" href="#peekator-managed-panel">
    Peekator sources respondents that match your target criteria. Ideal when you need to reach audiences you do not already have.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Your Own Panel" icon="database" href="#your-own-panel">
    You manage a pool of respondents and distribute the survey to them through Peekator. Full control over who is invited.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Peekator-managed panel

When you use the Peekator-managed panel, Peekator recruits respondents who match the demographic and screening criteria you define. This is useful for market research, product testing, or any study where you need to reach a specific audience you do not already have access to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Distribute tab">
    In your published survey, click **Distribute** and select **Panel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Peekator-managed panel">
    Select **Peekator Panel** as the panel source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your target audience">
    Set the screening and demographic criteria for your respondents, such as age range, country, gender, job title, or industry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sample size">
    Enter the number of completed responses you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Review the estimated cost and timeline, then confirm to launch the panel campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your own panel

If you already manage a respondent pool, you can connect it to Peekator and distribute surveys directly to your panel members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Distribute tab">
    In your published survey, click **Distribute** and select **Panel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your own panel">
    Select **My Panel** as the panel source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select respondents">
    Choose the panel members or segments you want to invite to this survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the invitation">
    Set up the invitation message and send time, then launch the distribution.
  </Step>
</Steps>
