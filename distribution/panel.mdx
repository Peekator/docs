Panel distribution is for when you want Peekator to find respondents on your behalf. You describe the audience you need, and the Peekator team sources participants from a managed panel, handles the launch, and monitors quality. This is useful when you do not have your own contact list or when you need a specific demographic that is hard to reach directly.

## Set up Panel distribution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Distribution">
    Inside your survey, go to **Setup** and select the **Distribution** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Panel">
    Choose **Panel** as your distribution channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the target details">
    Complete the following fields:

    - **No. of Respondents**: the target number of completed responses you want.
    - **Market**: the country or region where respondents should be located.
    - **Target Audience**: a description of the demographic or behavioural profile you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    After you save the Panel setup, the survey enters **Review** mode. The Peekator team reviews your requirements and manages the launch. You do not control the exact timing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after submission

Once you submit a Panel request:

- The survey moves to the **Review** state.
- The Peekator team validates your target audience and market.
- The team sources respondents and launches the survey when ready.
- Quality control is handled by the Peekator team throughout collection.

You can monitor progress from the survey dashboard, but you cannot start or stop collection yourself.

## When to use each channel

| Channel | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | You have your own audience and want full control over sharing. |
| **Campaign** | You have a contact list and want to send via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp. |
| **Panel** | You need Peekator to find respondents and manage the entire process. |
