## Choose a panel route

1. Open your survey and go to **Setup** → **Distribution**.
2. Select **Panel**.
3. Choose one of the following options:

| Option | Use it when |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Panel managed by Peekator** | You want Peekator to manage respondent recruitment and fieldwork. |
| **Connect Panel** | You are working with your own panel provider and want to connect it to Peekator. |

## Use a panel managed by Peekator

Select **Panel managed by Peekator** when you want Peekator to source respondents through its integrated panel network.

1. Enter the required **No. of Respondents**.
2. Select the **Market**.
3. Describe the **Target Audience**, including any key eligibility requirements, such as age, location, occupation or other screening criteria.
4. Select **Save changes**.
5. Select **Review and Launch**.

When you select **Review and Launch**, the survey moves to **In review**. The Peekator team checks the questionnaire and panel requirements, then launches the survey to the selected panels.

### After fieldwork

After data collection ends, the survey enters a **Data Quality** phase. The Peekator team reviews the responses and replaces any low-quality data where needed.

Once the data has been checked, the survey moves to **Done** with cleaned results.

## Connect your own panel provider

Select **Connect Panel** when you want to use an external panel provider.

1. Enter the required **No. of Respondents**.
2. Select **Add panel provider**.
3. Search for and select your provider from the list.
4. Select **Next Step**.
5. Enter the outcome IDs supplied by the panel provider.
6. Select **Save and Connect provider**.
7. Select **Save changes** on the Distribution page.
8. Select **Review and Launch** when the survey is ready.

You can add more than one provider if needed.

### Add provider outcome IDs

The panel provider supplies an ID for each respondent outcome. Enter these in the matching fields:

| Field | Use |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Success ID** | Sent when a respondent completes the survey successfully. |
| **Screenout ID** | Sent when a respondent does not meet the survey eligibility criteria. |
| **Quota full ID** | Sent when the relevant quota has already been filled. |
| **Quality control fail ID** | Sent when a respondent fails a quality-control check. |

<Note>
  Use the Quality control fail ID for respondents who fail quality checks. This lets the panel provider record the outcome as a quality failure rather than a standard screenout.
</Note>

## Before you launch

Check that:

- the respondent number is correct;
- the market and target audience are clear;
- all required quotas and screeners have been set up;
- the correct external provider is connected, where applicable; and
- the provider outcome IDs have been entered correctly.
