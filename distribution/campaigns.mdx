Campaigns let you reach your own contacts directly from Peekator. Upload or paste a contact list and send the survey via email, SMS, or WhatsApp. Each contact receives a unique survey link tied to their record, so their response is identified in your results.

## Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Distribute tab">
    In your published survey, click the **Distribute** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Campaigns">
    Click **Campaigns** and then **New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your contacts">
    Add the recipients for this campaign:

    - **Paste contacts**: type or paste a list of email addresses or phone numbers.
    - **Upload CSV**: upload a CSV file with columns for contact details (email, phone, first name, last name).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Select how you want to reach your contacts:

    - **Email**: respondents receive an email with a survey link.
    - **SMS**: respondents receive a text message with a short link.
    - **WhatsApp**: respondents receive a WhatsApp message with a survey link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose your message">
    Write the subject line (email) or message text (SMS/WhatsApp). Use `{{first_name}}` to personalize with the contact's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set send time">
    Choose **Send now** or **Schedule** to pick a specific date and time for delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Review the recipient count and click **Send campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up reminders

Peekator can automatically follow up with contacts who have not yet responded.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable reminders">
    In the campaign settings, toggle **Send reminder** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the delay">
    Choose how many days after the initial send to send the reminder. Common values are 3 or 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the reminder message">
    Edit the reminder text if you want different wording from the original message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Personalized messages using `{{first_name}}` improve open and response rates across all three channels.
</Tip>
