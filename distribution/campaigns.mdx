Before you create a campaign, set your survey’s distribution channel to **Campaign** and agree the sending setup with your Peekator account manager.

## Set campaign distribution

1. Open your survey and go to **Setup** → **Distribution**.
2. Select **Campaign**.
3. If needed, set the minimum number of days before a respondent can be contacted again. This helps prevent the same person from receiving invitations too frequently.
4. Select **Save changes**.

## Agree the sending channel

Before creating a campaign, tell your account manager:

- whether you want to send invitations by **email**, **SMS** or **WhatsApp**;
- the sender or domain you want to use;
- whether you would like to use a Peekator-managed sending domain or your own domain; and
- any requirements for the campaign template.

If you want to use a Peekator-managed sending domain, agree this with your account manager before the campaign is created.

If you want to send from your own domain, Peekator needs to complete some additional setup before it can be used. Allow a little extra time for this to be configured.

Once the channel has been set up, it becomes available to select in Campaign the mail navigation menu.

## Agree the campaign branding

Tell your account manager which branding you want to use for the campaign, including:

- your logo;
- brand colours; and
- any existing email template or styling requirements.

Campaign branding can be different from the branding used in the survey itself.

## Create the campaign

Once the distribution channel, sender and branding have been agreed:

1. Open **Campaign** in the main navigation.
2. Select **Campaigns** to open Campaign Hub.
3. Create and configure your campaign.
4. Upload your contact list and prepare the campaign message.
5. Review and launch the campaign when it is ready.

For detailed instructions, see [Campaign Hub](/campaigns/overview).
