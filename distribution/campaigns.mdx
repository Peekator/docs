Campaign distribution lets you send your survey directly to respondents through Email, SMS, or WhatsApp. You define the contact list and message content, and Peekator handles delivery and tracking. This is ideal for targeted outreach where you know exactly who you want to reach.

## Set up campaign distribution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Distribution">
    Inside your survey, go to **Setup** and select the **Distribution** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Campaign">
    Choose **Campaign** as your distribution channel. You are redirected to the Campaigns section for that survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the campaign">
    Set the channel (Email, SMS, or WhatsApp), upload or select your contact list, and compose the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set send delays">
    Use **CampaignTargetRespondentsDelayInDays** to control the delay between reminders or sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the campaign">
    Review your settings and launch. The campaign status updates as messages are sent and responses arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign statuses

Campaigns move through the following statuses during their lifecycle:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Campaign is created but not yet launched. |
| **Launched** | Campaign has been sent and is actively delivering. |
| **In Progress** | Messages are being sent and responses are being collected. |
| **Paused** | Campaign is temporarily stopped. |
| **Stopped** | Campaign has been manually halted. |
| **Done** | Campaign has completed its send schedule. |
| **Archived** | Campaign is no longer active and has been archived. |

<Tip>
  For a cross-project view of all campaigns, open **Campaign Hub** from the main menu next to the Peekator logo. This shows delivery status, response rates, and performance across every survey in one place.
</Tip>
