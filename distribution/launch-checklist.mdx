## Questionnaire

- [ ] The questionnaire includes all required questions, information screens and answer options.
- [ ] Question wording, instruction notes and answer options have been reviewed for clarity, spelling and tone.
- [ ] The survey has been tested from a respondent’s point of view using **Preview Survey** or the **Test Link**, where available.
- [ ] Logic, branching, piping and text substitutions work as expected.
- [ ] Any screeners have been tested, including the screenout path.
- [ ] Any quotas have been configured and tested.
- [ ] Required-answer settings and validation rules work as expected.
- [ ] Translations, if used, have been reviewed in every language.

## Survey settings

- [ ] The survey’s main language is correct.
- [ ] The welcome text and instruction text have been reviewed.
- [ ] The completion message and any end-of-survey redirect have been checked.
- [ ] The target number of responses is correct, where applicable.
- [ ] Any scheduled opening or closing dates have been set.
- [ ] Any survey messages, such as the screenout or quota-full message, have been reviewed.

## Distribution

- [ ] The correct distribution channel has been selected: **Link**, **Campaign** or **Panel**.

### Link

- [ ] The survey has been tested using the **Test Link**.
- [ ] You know how and where you will share the **Live Link**.
- [ ] You will share the Live Link only after the survey has been launched.

### Campaign

- [ ] The sending channel and branding have been agreed with the Peekator team.
- [ ] The correct contact list has been uploaded and mapped.
- [ ] Personalisation fields and any pre-filled survey answers have been checked.
- [ ] A test invitation has been sent and all placeholders display correctly.
- [ ] The campaign launch date and time are set more than 15 minutes in advance.

### Panel

- [ ] The required number of respondents is correct.
- [ ] The market and target-audience description are complete and accurate.
- [ ] All relevant screeners and quotas have been set up.
- [ ] If using an external panel, the correct provider is connected and all outcome IDs have been entered.

## Launch or submit for review

Follow the action for your selected distribution channel:

<table>
  <colgroup>
    <col width="173" />
    <col width="531" />
  </colgroup>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Distribution channel</th>
      <th>What to do next</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>**Link**</td>
      <td>Select **Review and Launch**, complete the final review and launch the survey. The survey then moves to **Collecting**.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Campaign**</td>
      <td>Complete the campaign setup in **Campaign Hub**, then review and launch the campaign from there.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Panel**</td>
      <td>Select **Review and Launch**. The survey moves to **In review**, and the Peekator team checks the setup before launching fieldwork.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

For surveys using the Peekator-managed panel, fieldwork is followed by a **Data Quality** phase. The Peekator team reviews responses and replaces low-quality data where needed before the survey moves to **Done**.
