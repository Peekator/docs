Before you click **Review and launch**, run through this checklist to make sure your survey is ready for respondents. Catching issues now prevents bad data, broken logic, or a poor respondent experience later.

## Questionnaire

- [ ] At least one question has been added to the questionnaire.
- [ ] Logic and branching have been tested using the **Test Link**.
- [ ] Screenout conditions have been tested to confirm disqualified respondents see the correct screenout message.
- [ ] Quotas have been tested if your survey uses them.
- [ ] Question text has been reviewed for spelling, clarity, and tone.

## Survey settings

- [ ] **IntroText** is set so respondents know what the survey is about.
- [ ] **OnCompleteText** is set so respondents see a clear thank-you message.
- [ ] **OnScreenoutText** is set if you are using screenout questions.
- [ ] **TotalNumberOfRespondents** matches your target sample size.
- [ ] **DateFrom** and **DateTo** are set if you want scheduled open and close times.

## Distribution

- [ ] At least one distribution channel is configured: **Link**, **Campaign**, or **Panel**.
- [ ] For link distribution, you have copied the **Live Link** and know how you will share it.
- [ ] For campaign distribution, your contact list and message content are ready.
- [ ] For panel distribution, your target audience description is accurate and complete.

## Final step

- [ ] Click **Review and launch**.

After you launch, the survey state changes:

- For **Link** and **Campaign** distribution, the survey may move directly to **Collecting**.
- For **Panel** distribution, the survey moves to **Review** so the Peekator team can manage the launch.
