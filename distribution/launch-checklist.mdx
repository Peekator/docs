Before you publish and distribute your survey, run through this checklist to catch common issues that are difficult or impossible to fix once responses start coming in.

<Warning>
  Once responses are collected, some settings cannot be changed without affecting data integrity. In particular, do not reorder or delete questions after you have received responses, as this can misalign existing response data.
</Warning>

## Survey content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview the survey on desktop">
    Click **Preview** in the builder and read through every question. Check for typos, unclear wording, and missing answer options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on mobile">
    Toggle to **Mobile** view in the preview bar. Confirm that questions, images, and buttons are readable and easy to interact with on a small screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check required questions">
    Verify that every question marked **Required** is intentional. Too many required questions increases drop-off rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test logic and branching">
    Walk through the survey choosing different answer combinations to confirm that skip logic, display logic, and end-of-survey rules all route correctly. See [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the redirect URL">
    If you set a post-submission redirect in [Survey settings](/survey-settings/overview), visit the URL to confirm it loads correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the response limit">
    If you set a response cap, confirm the number is correct for your research goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm start and end dates">
    If you set a survey window, double-check the start and end dates and times, including time zones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review notification settings">
    Confirm you will be notified appropriately, whether via per-response email or daily digest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Distribution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your distribution list">
    For campaign distribution, check your contact list for duplicates or outdated addresses before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the survey link">
    Copy the survey link and open it in an incognito/private browser window to confirm it loads correctly for an unauthenticated user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the embed (if applicable)">
    If you are using an inline or popup embed, check it on a staging or test version of your site before going live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    When everything looks correct, return to the survey builder and click **Publish**. Your survey is now live and ready to accept responses.
  </Step>
</Steps>
