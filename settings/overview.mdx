## My profile

Your profile page lets you update personal details and manage account security. To open it, click your name or initials in the top-right corner and select **Edit my profile**.

### Personal details

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| First name | Your given name as it appears in the platform |
| Last name | Your family name |
| Email | Your login email address |
| Client | The client account you belong to |
| Phone number | Optional contact number |

Click **Save** to apply changes or **Clear** to reset the form.

### Change password

Enter your current password and a new password to update your credentials. Passwords must meet the platform's minimum security requirements.

### Two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA to add an extra layer of security to your account. Once enabled, you can also:

- **Reset authenticator key** — unlink your current authenticator app and set up a new one
- **Generate recovery codes** — create one-time backup codes for account recovery if you lose access to your authenticator
