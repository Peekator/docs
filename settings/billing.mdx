The Billing section of your Peekator account shows your current subscription plan and lets you manage payment details and invoices. Contact your Peekator account manager or reach out to [support@peekator.com](mailto:support@peekator.com) for billing enquiries.

## Current plan

Your active subscription tier determines the features available to your organisation, the number of user seats, and the volume of survey responses included each month. Your plan details are shown at the top of the Billing page.

## Manage your subscription

To upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription, contact the Peekator team at [support@peekator.com](mailto:support@peekator.com). Enterprise and custom plans are available for organisations with specific requirements.

## Invoices

Past invoices are listed with the date, amount, and payment status. You can download individual invoices as PDF files for your records.

<Note>
  If you have questions about your invoice or need a specific billing document, contact [support@peekator.com](mailto:support@peekator.com).
</Note>
