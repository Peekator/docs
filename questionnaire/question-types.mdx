Peekator offers a wide range of question types organised into categories. Choose the right category for your research goals, then select the specific question type when adding questions to your survey or interview.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Standard" href="/questionnaire/question-types/standard">
    Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Grid, Numeric Grid, Numeric Entry, Ranking, NPS, Open End, Multiple Link Open End, Date, and Information Screen.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Translation" href="/questionnaire/question-types/translation">
    Language Single Choice — let respondents choose their preferred language at the start of the survey.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI" href="/questionnaire/question-types/ai">
    Conversational AI — interact with respondents using AI-driven dialogue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="UX Research" href="/questionnaire/question-types/ux-research">
    5 Second Test, Click Test, Card Sort, and Website Test — evaluate design and usability.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Market Research" href="/questionnaire/question-types/market-research">
    MaxDiff, Conjoint Analysis, Constant Sum, Quadrant Matrix, Pairwise Comparison, Upload File, Gabor Granger, and Van Westendorp.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Interview" href="/questionnaire/question-types/interview">
    Interview Topic — structure discussion topics for interview mode.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
