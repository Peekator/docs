## When to use Single choice

- You need respondents to select one answer only.
- Your answer choices do not overlap.
- You want to use the selected answer in logic or branching.

## Add a Single choice question

1. In the Questionnaire, select **Add question**.
2. Select **Single choice**.
3. Enter your question text.
4. An **Instruction note** is added automatically. Edit it if you need to give respondents additional guidance.
5. **Type** or **paste** your answer choices into the first answer field. 
6. Select **Add answer** to add further choices manually, or drag answers to change their order.

<Tip>
  You can **paste multiple answers** into the first field. Peekator automatically separates them into individual items.
</Tip>

## What respondents see

With **Radio** selected, respondents choose one visible answer. With **Dropdown** selected, they choose one answer from a dropdown list.

If you enable **Other**, respondents can enter their own answer after selecting it.

## Add conditions

You can add conditions to either the whole question or individual answer choices.

### Condition a question

A condition on a question controls whether respondents see the entire question. For example, show a question only to respondents who selected a particular answer earlier in the survey.

### Condition an answer

A condition on an answer controls whether a specific answer choice appears. For example, show a follow up answer option only when it is relevant to a respondent’s earlier response.

Use **Conditional rule on answer** in the Settings panel to add a condition to an individual answer.

<Note>
  For more information, see [Logic and branching](#).
</Note>

## Results in reporting

In reporting, you can see how many respondents selected each answer and the percentage each answer represents.

If you include an **Other** answer, its free text responses are available alongside the results and in exports.

## Example

Ask: “Which smartphone brand do you currently use?”

Answers: Apple, Samsung, Google and Other.
