Screening lets you end a survey early for respondents who do not meet your qualifying criteria. In Peekator, you do this with the **ScreenoutQuestion** type, which checks conditions and redirects ineligible respondents to a screenout message.

## How screening works

When a respondent answers a screenout question, Peekator evaluates the conditions you set. If the respondent does not match, the survey ends immediately and the respondent sees the **OnScreenoutText** configured in survey settings.

## Add a screenout question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add questions">
    Click **Add questions** in the questionnaire menu to open the question type selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Screenout">
    Choose the **ScreenoutQuestion** type (Filter respondents) and place it on the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the conditions">
    Define which answers qualify the respondent to continue. Respondents who select other answers will be screened out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the screenout text">
    Confirm the **OnScreenoutText** in survey settings says what you want ineligible respondents to see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** exact flow for setting screenout conditions and whether advanced quota-based screenouts exist.
</Note>

## When to use screenouts

- **Qualifying demographics:** ensure only target age groups, regions, or roles proceed
- **Removing ineligible respondents:** filter out those who do not use a product or meet a behaviour criterion
- **Protecting sample quality:** stop unqualified responses from entering your data set

## Test before launch

Always test screening logic before launching. Use the **test link** to walk through every answer path and confirm that:

- Qualifying answers let the respondent continue
- Non-qualifying answers trigger the screenout message
- The screenout text displays correctly
