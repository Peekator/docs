# Pairwise comparison question

Use a **Pairwise comparison** question to compare images two at a time and identify the preferred option.

Respondents choose between pairs of images. Their selection is saved, then another comparison appears.

## When to use Pairwise comparison

Use this question when you need to compare visual concepts, such as:

- packaging designs;
- advertisements;
- logo options;
- product images; or
- website or campaign creative.

It is especially useful when choosing from several alternatives would feel difficult in one screen.

## Add a Pairwise comparison question

1. In the questionnaire builder, select **Add questions**.
2. Choose **Pairwise comparison**.
3. Enter the question text.
4. Upload the images you want respondents to compare.

Select the upload area or drag and drop an image. Add every image you want to include as a possible answer.

<Info>
  An **Instruction note** is added automatically. You can edit it to give respondents additional guidance.
</Info>

## Conditional logic

Under **Ask only if**, select **Add conditional rule** to control whether the entire Pairwise comparison question is shown, based on an earlier answer.

For more information, see [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).

## What respondents see

Respondents see two images at a time and select the one they prefer.

After each selection, Peekator saves the choice and shows a new comparison. This continues until the respondent has completed the required comparisons.

## Results in reporting

Results show each image with the percentage of comparisons in which it was selected.

Use the results to identify:

- the strongest-performing visual option;
- options with similar appeal; and
- images that respondents selected least often.

## Example use case

A food brand has four packaging designs for a new range. It asks respondents to choose the design they prefer whenever two options are shown together.

The results reveal which packaging design performs best overall before the brand moves to final design development.
