Ranking questions ask respondents to arrange a list of items in order of preference by dragging and dropping. Use this when you need to understand relative priorities, not just whether an option is preferred.

<Tip>
  Keep ranking lists to 7 or fewer items to avoid cognitive fatigue and improve data quality.
</Tip>

## Add a ranking question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Question">
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add Question** and select **Ranking**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter question text">
    Type your question. For example: `Please rank the following features from most to least important.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add items to rank">
    Add each item as a sub-question. You can edit, remove, or reorder the list at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Items to rank** | Add, edit, remove, or reorder the list of items shown to respondents. Each item is a sub-question. |
| **Include "Other"** | Add an open-text "Other (please specify):" item that respondents can also rank. |
| **Limit ranked items** | Restrict how many items the respondent must rank. Set **Minimum options** and **Maximum options**. For example, "Rank your top 3" uses a maximum of `3`. |

<Note>
  The default instruction shown to respondents is: "Please rank the following options by dragging and dropping them into the desired order. Your top choice should be at the top of the list, and your least preferred choice should be at the bottom."
</Note>
