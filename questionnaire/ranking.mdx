## When to use a Ranking question

- You need respondents to compare several options directly.
- You want to identify the most and least preferred options.
- You want to understand the order of respondents’ priorities.

## Add a Ranking question

1. In the Questionnaire builder, select **Add question**.
2. Select **Ranking**.
3. Enter your question text.
4. An **Instruction note** is added automatically. Edit it if needed, for example to explain what the first and last positions mean.
5. Type or paste your answer choices into the first answer field. 
6. Select **Add answer** to add further choices manually, or drag answers to change their order.

<Tip>
  You can **paste multiple rows** or **columns** into the first field. Peekator automatically separates them into individual items.
</Tip>

## Add conditions and piping

Use **Add conditional rule** in the **Ask only if** section to control whether respondents see the Ranking question.

Select **Pipe answers** to use answers from earlier questions as ranking options.

For full instructions, see [Logic and branching](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9944be-99a0-83ed-acda-754df5c50a8c#) and [Piping answers](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9944be-99a0-83ed-acda-754df5c50a8c#).

## What respondents see

Respondents arrange the options in order, from their highest to lowest priority.

If you set an answer limit, they rank only the number of options you specify. Any remaining options are left unranked.

## Results in reporting

Ranking results show:

- The overall rank for each option.
- The average rank.
- The number of respondents who ranked the option.
- The percentage or count of respondents who placed the option in each position, such as first, second or third.

A lower average rank indicates a higher overall preference.

## Example

Ask: “Rank the following superpowers according to which one you would most like to have.”

Answers:

- Invisibility
- Mind reading
- Healing powers
- Time travel
- Shapeshifting
