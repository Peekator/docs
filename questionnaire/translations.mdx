Survey translations let you run a single Peekator survey in multiple languages. Respondents select their preferred language at the start, and all subsequent questions and text display in that language.

## Language selection

The **Language Single Choice** question (the **LanguageQuestion** type) is the entry point for multilingual surveys. It presents respondents with a list of available languages and routes them through the survey in their chosen language.

For details on configuring this question type, see the [Language Single Choice](/questionnaire/language-single-choice) guide.

## Set up translations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Language Single Choice question">
    Open the **Add questions** popup and select the **LanguageQuestion** type. Place it at the start of the questionnaire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define available languages">
    Choose the languages you want to offer to respondents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate survey content">
    Provide translated versions of question text, answer options, and any information screens for each language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test each language">
    Use the **test link** to walk through the survey in every language and verify translations display correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** exact workflow for adding language versions, whether translations are managed in the questionnaire builder or a separate screen, and which fields can be translated.
</Note>

## When to use translations

- **Multilingual markets:** reach respondents in their native language across regions
- **International panels:** support panel providers who field in multiple countries
- **Consistent analysis:** collect all responses in one survey for unified reporting, even when languages differ
