A Conversational AI question uses artificial intelligence to conduct a follow-up conversation with respondents based on their initial answer. The AI asks probing questions, clarifies responses, and gathers richer qualitative data automatically, reducing the need for manual interviewer follow-up.

## When to use this question type

- You want to collect deep qualitative insights at scale without human interviewers.
- You need to probe unclear or brief responses automatically.
- You want to explore topics where pre-defined follow-up questions would be too rigid.

## How to add it

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Conversational AI** from the popup.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    The question appears on the canvas. Click it to open the configuration panel and define the topic, initial question, and conversation parameters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Topic or prompt | The subject area the AI should explore with respondents. |
| Initial question | The first question the AI asks to start the conversation. |
| Maximum conversation length | The number of follow-up exchanges before the AI concludes. |
| Tone | The conversational style, for example, professional, friendly, or neutral. |
| End condition | A rule that ends the conversation early if the AI determines sufficient detail has been gathered. |

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** Confirm whether "Language restriction", "Voice input enabled", and "AI model selection" settings exist for this question type.
</Note>

## What respondents see

Respondents see a chat-style interface. They type or speak their answer to the initial question. The AI reads the response and asks a relevant follow-up. This continues until the maximum length is reached or the end condition is met. The experience feels like a text conversation rather than a traditional survey.

<Warning>
  This question type is only available in surveys, not interviews. Interview mode uses "Chat with Data" instead of "AI Analysis" for conversational features.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Conversational AI involves complex AI behaviour that can vary by response. Always preview and test with a range of sample answers before launching to ensure the conversation stays on topic and produces useful data.
</Warning>

## Logic and piping

You can use the presence or content of the AI conversation in logic to show or hide later questions. For example, if the AI tags a response as "highly negative", you can branch to a service recovery question. For full instructions, see [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).

## Results in reporting

Results appear as a transcript of each respondent's conversation. AI-generated summaries and themes are available in the dashboard. You can also export full transcripts for manual qualitative analysis or further coding.

## Example use case

A healthcare provider asks, "Tell us about your recent experience with our clinic." A respondent types, "It was okay." The AI follows up with, "What specifically went well, and what could have been better?" The conversation reveals detailed feedback about wait times and staff courtesy that a static survey would have missed.
