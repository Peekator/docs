Conversational AI questions use an AI assistant to ask respondents follow-up probing questions based on their previous answers. This creates an interview-style experience that captures deeper qualitative insights at scale without manually crafting every follow-up.

Use this type for exploratory research, customer discovery, brand perception studies, or any scenario where you need to probe beyond a single open-ended answer.

## Add a conversational AI question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Question">
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add Question** and select **Conversational AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the goal">
    Describe what the AI should try to uncover. The platform generates a prompt from this goal automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the maximum follow-ups">
    Set how many follow-up questions the AI can ask per respondent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the conversation">
    Use the Preview tab to test the AI conversation before launching.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Goal** | What the AI is trying to uncover through its follow-up questions. Used to auto-generate the prompt. |
| **Override prompt** | Enable to replace the auto-generated prompt with a fully custom one. |
| **Custom prompt** | The custom prompt sent to the AI when **Override prompt** is enabled. |
| **Max follow-up attempts** | Maximum number of follow-up questions the AI can ask per respondent. |
| **Do not mention specific suggestions** | Instructs the AI not to suggest specific answers, keeping responses more spontaneous. |
| **Save audio files** | Record and save the respondent's audio input alongside the text response. |

<Note>
  The default instruction shown to respondents is: "Our AI assistant will ask you a few follow-up questions."
</Note>

## What gets stored

Each conversational AI question stores three internal sub-answers per respondent:

- **Solution**: the final summarized answer the AI extracted
- **Full conversation**: the complete transcript of the AI-respondent conversation
- **Is success**: whether the AI determined it reached its goal

These fields are available in exports and the dashboard.
