## When to use this question type

Use a Conversational AI question when you want to:

- Understand the reasons behind a respondent’s answer.
- Explore experiences, opinions or unmet needs in more depth.
- Collect richer feedback than a single open ended response.

## Add a Conversational AI question

1. In the questionnaire builder, select **Add questions**.
2. Select **Conversational AI**.
3. Enter the opening question.
4. Add an optional **Instruction note** if respondents need guidance.
5. In **Goal**, describe what the AI should discover through its follow up questions.
6. Select **Save question**.

For example, for the question “What are the main reasons you prefer your chosen payment method?”, the goal could be: “Discover the benefits and concerns that influence the respondent’s preference.”

## Configure the question

Select **See more** in the settings panel to configure the conversation.

| Setting | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **No. of follow up attempts** | Sets the maximum number of follow up questions the AI can ask to achieve the goal. |
| **Do not mention specific suggestions** | Prevents the AI from referring to specific suggestions in its follow up questions. |
| **Save audio files to the server** | Saves respondents’ voice recordings. If this is not enabled, their spoken responses are kept as transcripts only. |

## Conditions

Use **Add conditional rule** under **Ask only if** to control whether the whole Conversational AI question is shown, based on an earlier answer.

For more information, see [Logic and branching](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9944be-99a0-83ed-acda-754df5c50a8c#).

## What respondents see

Respondents answer the opening question in their own words. The AI then asks relevant follow up questions, up to the number of attempts you set, to learn more about their response.

## Results in reporting

The Conversational AI dashboard provides a combined summary of all responses to the conversation.

You can also auto-code the responses in the same way as other open ended questions. This groups similar answers into categories, helping you identify themes across the responses.

For more information, see [Auto-code open ended responses](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9944be-99a0-83ed-acda-754df5c50a8c#).

## Example use case

A payment provider asks, “What are the main reasons you prefer your chosen payment method?” The AI follows up to understand whether the preference is driven by convenience, security, rewards, acceptance or another reason.
