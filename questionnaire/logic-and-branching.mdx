Logic rules in Peekator let you create a personalized experience by showing or hiding questions, skipping ahead, and routing respondents to different endings based on their answers. This reduces irrelevant questions and produces cleaner data.

<Note>
  Logic rules are evaluated top to bottom in the order they appear. If two rules conflict, the first matching rule takes effect.
</Note>

## Types of logic

- **Skip logic**: jump a respondent forward to a specific question based on their answer, bypassing questions in between.
- **Display logic**: show or hide a specific question only when a condition is met.
- **End-of-survey logic**: redirect or end the survey early based on an answer.

## Set up skip logic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Logic panel">
    In the questionnaire builder, click the **Logic** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Click **Add rule**, choose the source question, set the condition and answer value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the destination">
    Choose which question or section the respondent should jump to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Click **Save rule**. A logic indicator appears on the question card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up display logic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the question">
    Click the question you want to show or hide conditionally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Display Logic">
    In the settings panel on the right, scroll to **Display Logic** and click **Add condition**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define and apply the condition">
    Select the question to check, the operator, and the value. Click **Apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up end-of-survey logic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Logic panel">
    Click the **Logic** tab in the questionnaire builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an end rule">
    Click **Add end rule** and select the question, condition, and answer that triggers the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Select **End survey**, **Redirect to URL**, or **Disqualify**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always preview the questionnaire after adding logic to verify every answer path leads to the intended result.
</Warning>
