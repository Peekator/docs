Net Promoter Score (NPS) measures customer loyalty by asking how likely a respondent is to recommend a product, service, or brand. Peekator uses a fixed 0-10 scale and automatically calculates your NPS in the dashboard.

## How NPS is calculated

| Category | Score range | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Detractors** | 0-6 | Unhappy respondents who may discourage others. |
| **Passives** | 7-8 | Satisfied but unenthusiastic respondents. |
| **Promoters** | 9-10 | Loyal advocates who will recommend you. |

**NPS = % Promoters minus % Detractors**. Scores range from -100 to +100.

## Add an NPS question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Question">
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add Question** and select **NPS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the default question text">
    Peekator pre-fills: `On a scale of 0-10 how likely is it you would recommend XXXXXXX?` Edit it to reference your specific product or service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set endpoint labels">
    Add a label for the `0` end (for example, "Not at all likely") and for the `10` end (for example, "Extremely likely").
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Question text** | Customize to reference a specific product, service, or experience. |
| **Low-end label (0)** | Label displayed at the `0` end of the scale. |
| **High-end label (10)** | Label displayed at the `10` end of the scale. |

<Note>
  The scale is always 0-10 and cannot be changed. This ensures NPS scores are comparable across benchmarks.
</Note>
