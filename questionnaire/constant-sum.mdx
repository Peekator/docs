## When to use Constant sum

Use a Constant sum question when respondents need to allocate a fixed number of points, money or percentages across a list.

For example:

- divide a £1,000 travel budget between flights, accommodation and entertainment;
- distribute 100 points across product features based on importance; or
- show how a marketing budget should be shared between channels.

## Add a Constant sum question

1. In the questionnaire builder, select **Add questions**.
2. Choose **Constant sum**.
3. Enter the question text.
4. An **Instruction note** is added automatically. You can edit it to give respondents additional guidance.
5. Add the items respondents will allocate the value between.

<Tip>
  You can paste all items into the first answer field. Peekator separates them into individual answers automatically.
</Tip>

## Conditional logic

Under **Ask only if**, select **Add conditional rule** to control whether the entire Constant sum question is shown, based on an earlier answer.

For more information, see [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).

## What respondents see

Respondents see each item with a numeric field. They assign a value to every item until their combined allocation matches the total you set.

For example, if the total is 100, a respondent could allocate:

- 50 to accommodation;
- 30 to flight tickets; and
- 20 to entertainment.

## Results in reporting

Results show each item’s:

- **Average** allocated value;
- **Median** allocated value; and
- distribution of allocations across value ranges.

Use these results to identify which items receive the largest share of the available budget, points or percentage.

## Example use case

A travel company asks respondents to distribute £1,000 across accommodation, flight tickets and entertainment for a city break.

The results show the average amount respondents would allocate to each part of the trip, helping the company understand where customers place the most value.
