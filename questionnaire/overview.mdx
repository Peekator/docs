The **Questionnaire** is the first required step under **Setup**. You access it by clicking on any survey or interview card that is in Editing status from your project. Once inside, the page is divided into several zones described below.

## Navigation and breadcrumbs

At the top of the page, below the main Setup / Reporting menu, you will see a secondary breadcrumb bar showing:

**Survey** (or Interview tag) > **Setup** > **Questionnaire**

## Survey header

Below the breadcrumbs is the survey header row, which contains:

- **Survey name** — click the pen icon next to the name to edit it inline.
- **Project card** — a small rectangular card showing your project name. Click it to open a dropdown of all surveys and interviews in the project, so you can switch between them quickly.
- **Sample size** — shows `0` until you set the target sample in the [Distribution](/distribution/overview) tab.
- **Status tag** — shows the current status, for example **Editing**.

On the right side of the header:

- **Preview survey** — opens a respondent-facing preview of the questionnaire.
- **Export** — dropdown with the option to **Export questionnaire to PDF**.
- **Review and launch** — disabled until you have set up at least one question and one distribution channel.

<Note>
  If you set up **Link** as your distribution channel, a **Copy link** button appears between Export and Review and launch. Click it to copy either the **live link** or the **test link** from a dropdown.
</Note>

## Questionnaire menu

Below the survey header, a narrow left-side panel contains the Questionnaire menu. It sits just above the question list area and includes:

- **Hide menu icon** (lines with a left-pointing arrow) — collapses the Questionnaire menu to give more space to the question canvas.
- **Add questions** — opens a popup with all available question types. Click a type to add it to the questionnaire.
- **Options** — a dropdown with additional questionnaire-level settings.

## Question canvas

The main area to the right of the Questionnaire menu is where your questions appear. When the questionnaire is empty, you will see:

> _No questions to show — Start building your questionnaire by adding your first question_

Two actions are available from the empty state:

- **Add question** — same as the button in the Questionnaire menu.
- **Clone questionnaire** — copies the full question set from another survey into this one.

## Edit and preview tabs

Directly above the question canvas, two tabs let you switch between:

- **Edit question** (default) — the question builder view where you configure each question.
- **Preview question** — shows how the selected question will look to a respondent.

On the right side of the same row are two additional controls:

- **Set quota** — configure response quotas.
- **Set more** — a dropdown with additional question-level options .

## Next steps

Once you have added your questions, move to [Distribution](/distribution/overview) to choose how you will reach your respondents, then return to **Review and launch** to go live.
