The questionnaire builder is where you design the flow and content of a Peekator survey or interview. You reach it by clicking any survey or interview card that is in **Editing** status from the Projects and Campaigns menu.

<Tip>
  For interviews, this area is called the **Discussion guide** and behaves identically.
</Tip>

## Questionnaire header

At the top of the page you will find the survey name, a project switcher, the sample size, and the current status. From here you can also open **Preview survey**, **Export to PDF**, and the **Review and launch** button.

## Questionnaire menu

On the left, the menu contains:

- **Add questions** button to open the question type selector
- **Options** dropdown for additional actions
- **Hide menu** icon to collapse the sidebar

## Question canvas

The centre canvas is where questions are built and reordered. When empty, it shows shortcuts to **Add question** and **Clone questionnaire**.

Above the canvas are two tabs:

- **Edit question** — build and configure questions
- **Preview question** — see how a selected question renders to respondents

## Right-side controls

To the right of the canvas you will find:

- **Set quota** — configure respondent quotas
- **Set more** dropdown — additional survey-level settings

For details on each area, see the related guides below.
