## When to use an Interview topic

Use an Interview topic when you want to understand the reasons behind respondents’ answers, not just what they selected.

For example, use separate topics to explore:

- first impressions of a product;
- barriers to using a service;
- reactions to a campaign; and
- ideas for improvement.

## Add an Interview topic

1. In the questionnaire builder, select **Add questions**.
2. Choose **Interview topic**.
3. Enter a clear topic title.
4. Add the goals the AI should cover during the conversation.
5. Configure the tone and time limit.
6. Save the question.

<Note>
  Add another Interview topic whenever you want the AI to explore a separate subject. Keep each topic focused so the conversation remains natural and useful.
</Note>

## Set interview goals

Goals tell the AI what it should learn during the interview. Add between three and ten goals for each topic.

Select **Add goal** to write them manually.

You can also use **Goals helper**:

1. Describe what you want to learn in a few sentences.
2. Select **AI generate goals**.
3. Review and edit the suggested goals before saving.

Write specific, open-ended goals. For example:

- Understand what respondents liked most about the new service.
- Explore the reasons behind any frustration or confusion.
- Identify improvements that would make the experience more useful.

## Conditions and piping

You can add a condition to an Interview topic to control whether it is shown, based on an earlier answer.

Use **Add conditional rule** to define the condition. If it is not met, the topic is skipped.

Each Interview topic can have its own condition. This lets you run different follow-up conversations for different respondent groups.

You can also select **Pipe answers** to give the AI context from earlier survey responses, so it can ask more relevant follow-up questions.

For more information, see [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).

## What respondents see

Respondents take part in a natural conversation with the AI. The AI adapts its follow-up questions to the respondent’s answers while working through the goals you set.

Each topic starts a focused discussion. If your survey has multiple Interview topics, respondents complete each topic in turn.

## Results in reporting

Results include an **AI generated summary** for every respondent’s interview.

The summary gives a quick overview of what the respondent discussed. Select the response icon beside a summary to open the full interview response.

You can also use AI auto-coding in the same way as for other open-ended responses to group answers into themes and analyse them at scale.

## Example use case

A transport provider first asks respondents to rate a recent journey. It then adds two Interview topics:

1. **Journey experience** — to understand what worked well and what caused frustration.
2. **Future improvements** — to explore which changes would make respondents more likely to use the service again.

The first topic can use the rating as context, while the second focuses on practical suggestions.
