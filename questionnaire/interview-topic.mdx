An Interview Topic question defines a subject area or discussion theme for AI-led interviews in Peekator. It acts as a prompt that guides the artificial intelligence to explore a specific topic with the respondent, ensuring the conversation stays relevant and covers the areas you need for your research.

## When to use this question type

- You are running an AI-led interview and want to structure the conversation around specific themes.
- You need the AI to probe deeply on a topic while maintaining a natural, conversational flow.
- You want to combine the depth of qualitative interviews with the scale of automated data collection.

## How to add it

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Interview Topic** from the popup.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    The question appears on the canvas. Click it to open the configuration panel and enter the topic description, key sub-topics, and conversation style.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Topic title | The main subject the AI should discuss with the respondent. |
| Topic description | Guidance for the AI on what to explore, for example, "Discuss recent purchasing habits for luxury goods, including frequency, budget, and brand preferences." |
| Key sub-topics | Specific areas the AI should ensure it covers during the conversation. |
| Conversation style | The tone the AI should adopt, for example, professional, empathetic, or exploratory. |
| Maximum exchanges | The number of back-and-forth turns before the AI moves to the next topic. |
| End condition | A rule that ends the topic early if the AI determines sufficient detail has been gathered. |

<Note>
  **Needs product verification:** Confirm whether "Fallback question" and "Language restriction" settings exist for this question type.
</Note>

## What respondents see

Respondents see a chat interface where the AI introduces the topic and asks an opening question. The conversation proceeds naturally, with the AI probing based on the respondent's answers and the configured sub-topics. The experience feels like a text-based interview rather than a traditional survey.

<Warning>
  This question type is only available in Interview mode. Interview mode uses "Discussion guide" instead of "Questionnaire" and "Chat with Data" instead of "AI Analysis".
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Interview Topic questions involve complex AI behaviour that can vary by response. Always preview and test with a range of sample answers before launching to ensure the conversation stays on topic and produces useful data.
</Warning>

## Logic and piping

You can use the presence or content of the AI conversation in logic to show or hide later topics or questions. For example, if the AI tags a response as "highly satisfied", you can skip a follow-up satisfaction question. For full instructions, see [Logic and branching](/questionnaire/logic-and-branching).

## Results in reporting

Results appear as a transcript of each respondent's conversation for this topic. AI-generated summaries and themes are available in the dashboard under "Chat with Data". You can also export full transcripts for manual qualitative analysis or further coding.

## Example use case

A luxury travel agency creates an Interview Topic titled "Premium travel preferences". The AI asks about recent trips, budget, destination types, and booking behaviour. Key sub-topics include "Sustainability concerns" and "Loyalty programme usage". The conversation adapts based on whether the respondent mentions eco-friendly hotels or frequent flyer programmes.
