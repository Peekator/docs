Interview topic questions power AI-moderated interview conversations in Peekator. Each interview topic is a discussion area where the AI acts as an interviewer, asking follow-up questions to explore the topic in depth.

This question type is used in **Interview** mode (as opposed to standard Survey mode). The AI conducts the conversation based on your configuration and stores a full transcript and summarized answers per respondent.

## Add an interview topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Question">
    In the questionnaire builder, click **Add Question** and select **Interview Topic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the topic content">
    Write the main question or topic the AI should discuss with the respondent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interviewer tone">
    Choose **Professional** or **Casual** depending on the desired conversation style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally add a goal brief">
    Provide a brief that helps the AI understand what insights it should try to uncover during the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Interviewer tone** | **Professional**: formal, structured conversation. **Casual**: friendly, conversational style. |
| **Time limit** | Enable a time cap for this topic. Set the maximum duration in minutes. |
| **AI goal suggestion brief** | A brief description of what the AI should try to uncover, used to guide its follow-up questions. |

## What gets stored

Each interview topic stores three built-in answers per respondent:

- **Summarized answers**: the AI's summary of the key points from the conversation
- **Full conversation**: the complete AI-respondent transcript
- **Uploaded video**: any video recording attached by the respondent
