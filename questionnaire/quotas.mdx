Quotas let you control how many respondents with a given characteristic are accepted into your survey. They are essential for demographic balancing and ensuring your sample stays representative.

## What quotas are

A quota is a limit on the number of respondents who can select a specific answer or combination of answers. Once a quota cell is full, new respondents who match that cell are stopped from completing the survey.

## How to access quotas

Click the **Set quota** button located above the question canvas on the right side. This opens the quota configuration panel.

## Configure a quota cell

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Set quota">
    Click **Set quota** above the question canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the question">
    Select the question that defines the characteristic you want to limit, such as age group or region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the answer">
    Pick the specific answer option that forms the quota cell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the target count">
    Enter the maximum number of respondents you want to accept for that cell.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens when a quota is full

When a respondent matches a full quota cell, the survey ends and the respondent sees the **OnQuotaFullText** configured in survey settings.

## When to use quotas

- **Demographic balancing:** ensure equal or weighted representation across age, gender, or region
- **Representative samples:** prevent any one group from overfilling your data set
- **Panel management:** control how many completes you accept from specific segments

## Test before launch

Always test quota logic before launching. Use the **test link** to simulate responses and confirm that:

- Quota counts increment correctly
- Full quotas trigger the quota-full message
- The **OnQuotaFullText** displays as expected
