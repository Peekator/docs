Peekator's export tools let you take your survey data out of the platform for further analysis, sharing with stakeholders, or archiving. You can export raw response data, formatted reports, and crosstab tables in several file formats, and schedule recurring exports to be delivered by email automatically.

<Note>
  Active filters carry over to exports. If you have filters applied in the dashboard when you start an export, only the filtered subset of responses is included. Remove all filters first if you want a full export.
</Note>
