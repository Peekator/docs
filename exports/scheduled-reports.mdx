Peekator lets you export your survey data in several formats so you can analyse results in external tools, share findings with stakeholders, or archive your research. You can access exports from the dashboard or from the **Export** button in the questionnaire header.

## Available export formats

### CSV

CSV exports contain response-level data in a plain text format suitable for spreadsheets and data pipelines. Variants may include:

- With or without respondent IDs
- Filtered results only, if you have applied dashboard filters before exporting

Use CSV when you need raw data for custom analysis or import into another system.

### Excel

Excel exports include calculated results and cross-tabulations in a formatted workbook. This is ideal when you want summaries and pivot-ready tables without building them yourself.

Use Excel for quick summaries and when you need to share structured results with colleagues who prefer spreadsheets.

### SPSS

SPSS exports are provided as `.sav` or `.zip` files compatible with statistical software such as IBM SPSS Statistics. Variable labels and value labels are preserved where possible.

Use SPSS when you need to perform advanced statistical analysis on your survey data.

### PowerPoint

PowerPoint exports generate a chart deck using the visuals from your dashboard. Each question typically becomes a slide with its corresponding chart.

Use PowerPoint when you need to present findings to stakeholders or include charts in a slide deck.

### PDF

PDF exports produce a formatted report of your survey results, including charts and tables, in a single document.

Use PDF for sharing a clean, read-only report that does not require any special software to open.

## How to choose an export format

| Goal | Recommended format |
| --- | --- |
| Raw data for custom analysis | CSV |
| Summarised results with calculations | Excel |
| Statistical modelling | SPSS |
| Presentation to stakeholders | PowerPoint |
| Shareable read-only report | PDF |
