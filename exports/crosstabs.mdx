Crosstabs, or cross-tabulation tables, show how different groups of respondents answered each question in your survey. Instead of viewing overall results, you can break responses down by a demographic or segment to spot differences in behaviour, preference, or opinion across groups.

## When to use crosstabs

Use crosstabs when you need to compare results across respondent groups. Common use cases include:

- **Demographic breakdowns**: see how age groups, regions, or genders differ in their answers
- **Audience segment comparison**: compare responses from customers vs non-customers, or from different campaign channels
- **Quality control**: check whether certain segments are skewing your overall results

## How crosstabs are structured

A crosstab table typically places:

- **Rows**: the answer options for a survey question
- **Columns**: the groups you are comparing, such as a demographic variable or segment
- **Cells**: the count or percentage of respondents in each group who selected each answer

This layout makes it easy to scan across columns and compare how each group responded to the same question.
