Peekator helps you design surveys and interviews, reach your audience through multiple channels, and analyse results with dashboards, crosstabs, and AI-powered tools. Whether you are running a quick poll or a large-scale research programme, Peekator guides you from project setup to actionable insights.

## Your client journey

Most research projects on Peekator follow six simple stages. You can move between stages at any time, and the platform keeps your work safe as you go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Start a new project to organise your research. Every project can hold multiple surveys and interviews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a survey or interview">
    Choose between a standard survey or an interview. Interviews use a discussion guide and offer chat-based analysis instead of the standard questionnaire builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your questionnaire or discussion guide">
    Add questions, set logic and branching, configure quotas, and preview how respondents will experience your research.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    Set your sample size, review quality control options, and adjust any survey-specific preferences before launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute">
    Share your survey via a direct link, an email or SMS campaign, or a managed panel. Each channel gives you different levels of control over respondent targeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyse">
    Watch responses arrive in real time, explore dashboards and crosstabs, and use AI features to surface themes and summaries automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the docs are organised

The documentation is arranged into five tabs so you can find what you need quickly:

- **Start Here**: welcome, quickstart, and account setup.
- **Projects**: creating and managing projects, surveys, and interviews.
- **Build**: questionnaire design, logic, quotas, and preview tools.
- **Distribute**: link sharing, campaigns, and panel management.
- **Analyse**: dashboards, crosstabs, exports, and AI-powered analysis.
