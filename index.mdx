Peekator is a research platform for creating surveys and interviews, distributing them to your audience, and analyzing results with dashboards, crosstabs, and AI-powered insights.

When you log in, you land on the **Projects** page — your central workspace for all research activity. From there you create projects, add surveys and interviews, and access all setup and reporting tools.
