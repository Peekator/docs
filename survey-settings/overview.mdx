Use **Setup** → **Settings** to manage survey settings.

## Basic settings

Everyone can view and edit these settings.

### Set the main survey language

Use **Main Language of the Survey** to select the survey’s original language. English is selected by default.

For translated surveys, this is the language that all translations are based on. See [Survey translations](/questionnaire/translations).

### Let respondents go back

Enable **Enable Back Button** to let respondents return to previous questions.

## Set respondent messages

Use **Messages** to edit the text respondents see at key points in the survey.

| Message | When respondents see it |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Introduction message** | On the welcome screen before the survey starts. |
| **Success message** | After completing the survey. |
| **Screenout message** | When they are screened out. |
| **Quota full message** | When the relevant quota is full. |

Use the editor toolbar to format text and add links or media.

## Configure basic exports

Enable **Show SPSS codes on all questions** to include SPSS codes for every question in your survey export.

## Add context for better AI

Add your research and business goals to help Peekator AI interpret your survey results.

| Field | What to add |
| :-- | :-- |
| **What’s your research goal of the project** | The research question you want the survey to answer. |
| **What’s your business goal of the project** | The business decision or outcome the research should support. |

For example:

- Research goal: _What would be an acceptable price range for our new product in the UK market?_
- Business goal: _Decide which price point and positioning to use for launch._

This context helps improve AI-generated summaries, analysis and auto-coding.

<Note>
  Save each settings section separately after making changes.
</Note>

## Advanced settings

Advanced settings are available only when you distribute your survey through a link, campaign or your own panel.

### Manage collection

Use **Survey Status** to control whether the survey is collecting responses. Check this before sharing the survey.

### Add more information to exports

If available, you can also include:

| Setting | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Show time spent on each question** | Includes the time each respondent spent on every question. |
| **Show length of interview** | Includes each respondent’s total interview length. |

### Show the estimated survey length

Enable **Show average respondent time** to display the estimated completion time to respondents.

Enter the **Average expected time for the survey completion** in minutes.

### Check for speeders

Use these settings to identify respondents who complete the survey too quickly:

1. Enter the expected completion time.
2. Set the **Percentage cutoff time**.
3. Enable **Turn on QC for speeders**.

For example, if the expected completion time is 6 minutes and the cut-off is 30%, the speeder threshold is 1.8 minutes. Responses completed faster than this are assessed by the speeder quality-control rule.

### Control survey flow

| Setting | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Allow continuing the survey** | Lets respondents return to a partially completed survey. |
| **Redirect survey at the end** | Sends respondents to another URL after they complete the survey. |
| **Skip welcome screen** | Sends respondents directly to the first question instead of showing the introduction message. |
| **Require respondents to click Continue after selecting an answer** | Requires respondents to confirm their selection before moving on. |

### Brand your survey

Use **Custom CSS for this survey** for white-label branding.

To add your logo or change the survey colours to match your brand, contact the Peekator team. We can configure the styling for you.
