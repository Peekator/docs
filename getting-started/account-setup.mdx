When your account is created, a member of the Peekator team sends your initial login details by email. Use these details to sign in to Peekator for the first time.

## Sign in for the first time

1. Open the Peekator login page.
2. Enter the email address and password shared by the Peekator team.
3. Select **Log in**.

After you have logged in, you can update your profile and change your password at any time.

If you no longer have your login details or cannot access your account, contact the Peekator team.

## Open your account menu

Your name and initials appear in the upper-right corner of every page. Click on them to open the account menu.

## Update your profile

Click **Edit my profile** to open the **My profile** page.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 12 36](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.12.36.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 12 36")
</Frame>

Use the **Basic info** section to review and update your personal details.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| First name | Your first name |
| Last name | Your last name |
| Email | The email address you use to sign in |
| Client | The organisation you belong to. This is assigned automatically. |
| Phone number | Optional contact number |

Click **Save** to apply any changes, or **Clear** to reset the form.

### Change your password

On the **My profile** page, select **Change password** in the **Password** section.

Enter your new password, confirm it, then save your changes.

For security, change the initial password shared with you after signing in for the first time.

### Set up two factor authentication

Two factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. Once enabled, you will be asked for a code from your authenticator app each time you sign in.

1. Open **My profile** from the account menu.
2. In the **Two factor authentication** section, enable 2FA.
3. Scan the QR code using an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
4. Enter the verification code when prompted to complete the setup.

<Note>
  Keep your recovery codes somewhere secure. You may need them to access your account if you lose or replace your device.
</Note>

After two factor authentication is enabled:

- Use **Reset authenticator key** to connect a new authenticator app or change devices.
- Use **Generate recovery codes** to create backup codes in case you lose access to your authenticator app.

Keep your recovery codes somewhere secure. You may need them to access your account if you lose or replace your device.
