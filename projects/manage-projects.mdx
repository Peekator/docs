All project management actions are available directly from the project card on the homepage. Use the project title or the three-dot menu to rename, delete, or manage the surveys inside a project.

## Rename a project

You can rename a project in two ways:

- **Click the project title** directly on the card. The title becomes an editable text field. Make your changes, then click the checkmark to save or the X to cancel.
- **Three-dot menu**: click the ⋮ menu on the project card and select **Rename Project**. The same inline edit activates.

## Delete surveys inside a project

To remove one or more surveys from a project without deleting the whole project:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    Click the ⋮ menu on the project card and select **Select Surveys to Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select surveys">
    The survey cards enter selection mode. Tick the surveys you want to remove.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 09 03 At 14 58 33 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-14.58.33-1.png "Screenshot 2026 09 03 At 14 58 33 1")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Confirm the deletion. Deleted surveys and their response data cannot be recovered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a project

<Warning>
  Deleting a project permanently removes all surveys and their data inside it. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    Click the ⋮ menu on the project card and select **Delete Project**.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 09 03 At 15 01 06](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-15.01.06.png "Screenshot 2026 09 03 At 15 01 06")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Delete** to proceed or **X** to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Load more projects

The homepage shows a set number of project cards at a time. If you have more projects, click **Show more** at the bottom of the page to display additional cards.
