You can rename projects, remove individual surveys or interviews from a project, or delete an entire project from the Projects homepage. All management options are available from each project card.

## Rename a project

You can rename a project in two ways:

1. **Inline**: click the project name directly on the card. A text field appears with a checkmark to confirm and an X to cancel.
2. **Three-dot menu**: click **⋮** on the project card, then select **Rename Project**.

## Select surveys to delete

To remove surveys or interviews from a project without deleting the project itself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click **⋮** on the project card, then choose **Select Surveys to Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select items">
    Checkboxes appear on each survey and interview card. Tick the ones you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Confirm the deletion. The selected items are removed from the project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  This action removes the selected surveys or interviews permanently. It cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Delete a project

To remove an entire project and all its contents:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click **⋮** on the project card, then choose **Delete Project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A simple confirmation modal appears with **Cancel** and **Delete** buttons. Click **Delete** to proceed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a project removes all surveys, interviews, and data inside it. This action is permanent.
</Warning>

## Load more projects

When you have more than 24 projects, a **Load more projects** button appears at the bottom of the homepage. Click it to load the next batch of 24 projects.
