Projects are the starting point for all your research in Peekator. Every survey and interview lives inside a project. When you log in, the Homepage shows all your existing projects and lets you create new ones.

![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 35 25](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.35.25.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 35 25")

## Create a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create new project">
    From the Homepage, click **Create new project** in the upper right corner.

    ![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 37 34](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.37.34.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 37 34")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a project name">
    Type the name of your project in the field provided.

    ![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 36 22](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.36.22.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 36 22")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable AI context (optional)">
    Toggle **Context for better AI** to enable it. When on, you can enter the research and business goals of this project. The AI uses this context to provide more tailored results when you use AI features in analysis. This step is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create project">
    Click **Create project**. Your new project appears as a card on the Homepage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The project card

Once created, your project appears as a card on the Homepage. Each project card shows:

![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 41 09](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.41.09.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 41 09")

- **Project name** — click the name directly to rename it.
- **Start date** — set when the project was created. The end date appears once data collection is finished.
- **Add survey** and **Add interview** buttons to start adding research activities.
- A **three-dot menu** (⋮) for renaming, deleting surveys, and deleting the project. See [Manage projects](/projects/manage-projects) for details.

## Add a survey or an AI interview to your project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add survey or Add interview">
    On the project card, click **Add survey** or **Add interview**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 30 12](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-02-at-16.30.12.png "Screenshot 2026 09 02 At 16 30 12")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a survey or an interview name">
    Type the name of your survey or interview in the placeholder field and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once created, the survey or interview appears as a card inside the project card, showing:

- **Survey or interview name**
- **Tag**: Survey or Interview
- **Status**: Editing
- **Sample size**: 0 (updated as responses are collected)

The project card displays up to 4 survey cards at a time. Click **Show more** below the cards to see additional surveys.

To add more surveys or interviews later, click **Add more** below the survey cards and choose **Add Survey** or **Add Interview**.

![Add more dropdown showing Add Survey and Add Interview options](/images/project-add-more.png)

## Open a survey

Click on any survey card to open the questionnaire builder, where you can start building your questions.
