When you log in to Peekator, the first thing you see is the **Projects** page. This is your central workspace. Every research project you run lives here as a card, showing its surveys, interviews, and current status at a glance.

From the Projects page you can create new projects, open existing ones, and navigate to the Campaigns page using the main menu next to the Peekator logo.
