Projects are the top-level containers for all research in Peekator. Every survey and interview lives inside a project, so organising your work starts here. After you log in, the Projects homepage shows all your projects as cards. You can also reach Projects from the main menu at any time.

## What you can do with projects

From the Projects homepage you can create new projects, add surveys or interviews to existing projects, and manage project settings. Projects help you keep related research together, whether you are running a single survey or a full campaign with multiple interviews.

## Project list and pagination

Projects load in batches of 24 on the homepage. If you have more than 24 projects, a **Load more projects** button appears at the bottom of the page. Click it to reveal the next batch.
