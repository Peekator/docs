Every piece of research in Peekator starts as either a survey or an interview inside a project. Surveys and interviews use different tools and terminology, so choose the mode that matches your research method.

## Survey vs interview

| Feature | Survey | Interview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | Questionnaire | Discussion guide |
| AI analysis | AI Analysis | Chat with Data |

Use **Survey** when you want respondents to complete a structured questionnaire on their own. Use **Interview** when you want a conversational, guided discussion format.

## Add your first survey or interview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a project">
    On the Projects homepage, find the project where you want to add research. If the project is new, the project card shows **Add survey** and **Add interview** buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Click **Add survey** or **Add interview**, depending on the mode you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type a name for the survey or interview, then confirm. A new card appears on the project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add more surveys or interviews

If a project already contains surveys or interviews, click **Add more** below the existing survey cards. A popup appears with two options: **Add Survey** or **Add Interview**. Choose one and confirm the name.

## The survey or interview card

Each card shows:

- **Name**: the title you gave the survey or interview
- **Tag**: **Survey** or **Interview**
- **Status**: starts as **Editing**
- **Sample size**: starts at **0**

<Note>
  Click any survey or interview card to open the questionnaire builder or discussion guide editor.
</Note>
