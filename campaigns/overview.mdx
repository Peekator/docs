The **Campaigns** page is accessible from the main menu next to the Peekator logo, alongside Projects. It gives you a cross-project view of all distribution campaigns you have sent or scheduled, including email, SMS, and WhatsApp campaigns.

Use the Campaigns page to monitor delivery status, response rates, and campaign performance across all your surveys in one place.

For setting up a new campaign for a specific survey, see [Distribution via Campaigns](/distribution/campaigns).
