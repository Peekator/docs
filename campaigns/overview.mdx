The **Campaign Hub** is a cross-project view of all your distribution campaigns. Open it from the main menu next to the Peekator logo to see delivery status, response rates, and performance across every survey in one place. This is different from setting up a campaign for a single survey, which you do inside the **Distribution** tab of that survey.

## What you can do in Campaign Hub

- Monitor delivery status for Email, SMS, and WhatsApp campaigns.
- Track response rates and campaign performance across all projects.
- See which campaigns are active, paused, or completed.

## Campaign channels

Campaigns in Peekator support three channels:

| Channel | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Send survey invitations and reminders to a list of email addresses. |
| **SMS** | Send survey links via text message to mobile numbers. |
| **WhatsApp** | Send survey invitations through WhatsApp to opted-in contacts. |

## Campaign statuses

Campaigns in the hub display one of the following statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Campaign is created but not yet launched. |
| **Launched** | Campaign has been sent and is actively delivering. |
| **In Progress** | Messages are being sent and responses are being collected. |
| **Paused** | Campaign is temporarily stopped. |
| **Stopped** | Campaign has been manually halted. |
| **Done** | Campaign has completed its send schedule. |
| **Archived** | Campaign is no longer active and has been archived. |

<Note>
  To create a new campaign for a specific survey, go to the **Distribution** tab inside that survey.
</Note>
